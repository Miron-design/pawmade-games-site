Purrfect Miners (the "Game")

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

The Game is published by PawMade Games ("we", "us"), a brand operated by Miron Strzałkowski, a sole proprietor, ul. Bukowa 79, 41-600 Świętochłowice, Poland. You can contact us at miron.strzalkowski@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

The Game stores your progress on your device. In addition, the services below process some data when you play.

  • Game analytics (Firebase Analytics, Google). Gameplay events (for example levels started, completed or quit, boosters used, tutorial steps, in-game purchases of virtual items), your player level, and technical information about your device: device model, operating system and version, screen size, memory, graphics processor, language, and the app version. Errors that occur in the Game may be logged as well. Google also assigns a random app installation identifier. Analytics is only sent after you give consent.
  • Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config, Google). The Game downloads game settings such as prices, offers and level balance. For this, Google receives a random installation identifier, the app version, the operating system and your IP address.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob). The Game can show rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. To serve ads, Google may process your advertising identifier, IP address and device information. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are only requested according to your choice in the consent form shown by Google's User Messaging Platform.
  • Purchases. Real-money purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the Game schedules local reminders (for example when your lives are full). They are created on your device and are not sent from a server.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name or email address through the Game.

3. Why we process it and on what basis

  • To understand how the Game is played and to improve it: analytics, based on your consent.
  • To show advertising and measure it: based on your consent where the law requires it.
  • To deliver game settings and keep the Game working: our legitimate interest in operating the Game.

4. Who receives the data

Data is processed by Google (Firebase and AdMob) on our behalf or as an independent controller for advertising. We may export analytics data to Google BigQuery for analysis, stored in the EU region. We do not sell your personal data.

5. International transfers

Google may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, using safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or other mechanisms described in Google's terms.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the Game ([SETTINGS → PRIVACY SETTINGS, ADD THIS BUTTON BEFORE RELEASE]).

  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us at the address above. Because we do not know who a random installation identifier belongs to, we may ask for it to find your data.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
